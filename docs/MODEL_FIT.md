Generated by `cargo run --release -p sim-core --example fit_check -- <cmodel>...` (kinematic core, no MuJoCo).

For every cmodel and built-in scenario the robot outline **plus the actuator envelope** (fork tines, reach) is swept along every topology lane (heading along the lane, both directions, 0.1 m steps) and rotated through 360° at every node with ≥ 2 lanes, against walls/shelves (*static*) and again with the warehouse dressing (`SIM_DRESS=auto`).

## Reading the table

* **Jacking, tugger, 12345 (no top module)** fit every scenario.
* **forklift_4steer** (rotate Ø 3.0 m, 2.07 m swept radius with forks) does not fit any built-in scenario: lanes end 1.5 m from the walls and turning circles overlap the shelf blocks. It needs a dedicated wide-aisle layout.
* **pallet_truck_amr** (1.15 m tines on a 0.85 m body) fits only `rect_loop` and `fms_workshop_xl`; in the 9-grid / cross / narrow layouts the tine tips reach the walls at lane ends.
* **03_AOBO reach truck** and **测试车模型 (rear-fork stacker)** fit grid / cross / loop / XL but not `narrow_aisle` or `fms_workshop`.
* **roller_amr**: the residual `+dress R` entries are station nodes, where the conveyor sits 4 cm beside the docked robot — the robot must arrive aligned and cannot turn in place there (this is how a real roller dock works).

## Fixes that came out of the check

* Dressing keeps the robot's **turning circle** free at every node (`node_clear = max(lane_clear, swept_r + 0.15)`; swept_r covers body, fork tines and a carried jacking rack). Before: up to 13 nodes per scenario blocked only by props.
* Roller **conveyor stations** are placed on the side of the docking pose that is clear of crossing lanes (left, then right), and never at charging / standby / dispatch stations. Before: conveyors sat on crossing lanes (28 lane hits in `grid_9_square`).
* Jacking / roller **deck height** = chassis body top, not the cmodel envelope height (a 2.03 m envelope gave a 2.03 m tray).
* **测试车模型** (single steer wheel ahead of the load axle, ZAPI pump `lift-motor`, 2.03 m, no fork module) is now classified as a **stacker with rear-facing forks** (`fork_dir = -1`, tine tips at the tail) instead of a jacking robot. `fork_dir` can be overridden in the model's `actuator` override.
